FAQ

Vegas questions.

Do you do trade show booths?

It's one of our favourite rooms. The organizers of InfoComm 2026 placed our wall at the entrance of the venue as part of every attendee's intro journey — hundreds of people painted daily, and attendees kept coming back to say it beat everything on the floor. ComplexCon, NACS, and convention booths of every size: the wall stops traffic and holds it.

Do you have a local crew in Las Vegas?

Yes. Local writers work with us in Vegas, and our gear moves through the city constantly — F1, ComplexCon, InfoComm and more. It's one of our most active markets.

Can it run outdoors on the Strip?

Yes. The LED configuration is built for desert daylight — the F1 Fan Experience wall ran right on the Strip. For indoor ballrooms and booths, LCD and projection setups keep the footprint tight.

How far in advance should we book?

4–6 weeks is comfortable. For major show weeks — CES, F1, ComplexCon — book as early as you can; Vegas dates fill fast.
