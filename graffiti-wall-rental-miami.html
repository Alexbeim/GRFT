FAQ

Miami questions.

Do you have a local crew in Miami?

Yes. Local Miami writers work with us — from the city that gave graffiti Wynwood Walls. They staff the wall, paint alongside guests, and keep the culture front and centre.

Can it handle Florida heat and sun?

Yes. The LED configuration is built for exactly this — full sun, outdoor fanzones, all-day festival crowds. Rolling Loud and the Daytona 500 both ran outdoors without breaking a sweat.

Is it right for festivals?

It's where the wall shines. High footfall, zero barrier — anyone can pick up a can and paint within seconds, and the crowd around the painters becomes part of the show. Ask us about multi-day festival runs.

How far in advance should we book?

4–6 weeks ahead is comfortable for Miami and Florida events. For festival season and Art Basel week, book early — those dates go first.
