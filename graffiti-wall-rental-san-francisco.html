FAQ

Bay Area questions.

Do you have a local crew in San Francisco?

Yes. Local Bay Area writers work with us — they staff the wall, guide guests through their first pieces, and bring the Bay's own style to the work.

Is it right for conferences and product launches?

Very. The wall gives conference crowds something to make, not just watch — and launches get a canvas guests genuinely want to share. Nike used ours to put custom art on 3D Air Jordans; yours can carry whatever you're launching.

Which configuration fits an SF venue?

Depends on the room. LED for Moscone-scale floors and outdoor plazas, LCD for offices and retail, projection for moody warehouse venues. Send us the space and we'll recommend the right call.

How far in advance should we book?

4–6 weeks ahead is comfortable. Our West Coast base makes SF logistics easy — shorter timelines are often workable.
