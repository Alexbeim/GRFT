InfoComm invited us in and put us at the front door — the first thing every attendee saw before they reached the floor. On an Absen LED wall, thousands picked up a can, and the phrase we kept hearing was that it was the most innovative thing at the show.

InfoComm didn't slot us into a back aisle. They sought us out as a hands-on experience worth leading with, and placed the wall right at the entrance — so every person walking into the show met a live graffiti wall before they met a single booth. For a room full of pro-AV people who build screens for a living, that's the position you want.

The wall ran in partnership with Absen, whose LED made the canvas look exactly the way it needed to — a brick wall bright enough to hold its own in a convention-center concourse, close enough to the real thing that people did a double-take. Graffiti+ × Absen, front and center.

Over the run, thousands of attendees stepped up to try it. They painted, they asked how it worked, they traded cards — and the reaction repeated itself all week: the most original, most talked-about thing on the floor. Those conversations turned into real connections with large brands and genuine potential for new projects — the kind of pipeline a show like this is actually for.

We're grateful for the invitation, and especially for the support and enthusiasm of Dani Dade, who championed getting us there and set the whole thing up to win. And to close the show, we brought in a special guest: Gear Duran of Futuriscape, who sent it off with a standout piece on the wall.

Eighteen years. Six continents. One idea, done right.

For eighteen years, Graffiti+ has been putting real creative tools in people's hands — in public, at scale, and always with cultural integrity.

Born from the roots of graffiti culture and built for anyone willing to pick up a custom spray can, the platform turns trade-show floors and brand activations into shared canvases where writers and first-timers paint together in real time. From their home in Vancouver to projects across six continents, Graffiti+ has partnered with brands including Nike, Samsung, Jabil, Johnson & Johnson and Dell to bring authentic, participatory experiences to audiences everywhere.

Put a live graffiti wall at the front door, and the whole show stops on its way in.
