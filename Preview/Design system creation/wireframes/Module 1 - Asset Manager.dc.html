Step 01 · First time here

Welcome. Let's build your wall.

You're setting up the interactive graffiti wall for the Brewery Launch Party. Everything your guests will paint with — your logo, your colors, your spray cans, your stencils — gets built right here, by you.

It takes about ten minutes. Eight steps, in order. Nothing goes live until you approve it at the end, and you can jump back to any step before then. No design software, no back-and-forth — what you build is what lands on the wall.
