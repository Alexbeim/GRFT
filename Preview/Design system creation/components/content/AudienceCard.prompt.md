Hairline-separated content blocks — `AudienceCard` (who it's for) and `NumberedStep` (01–04 how-it-works). Never rounded accent-border cards; rules divide, they don't box.

```jsx
<AudienceCard label="Brands" value="Turn a launch into a live, photographable moment." />
<NumberedStep number="01" title="Load in," second="we take the wall."
  body="From permits to last tag, the site is ours." />
```

Title is Archivo 800; muted second line; body Archivo 400. Stack several across a row with `gap`.
