Big-figure proof block. Wrap 3–4 `Stat`s in a `StatRow` for the credibility row. At most one `accent` figure.

```jsx
<StatRow>
  <Stat figure="18" label="Editions" />
  <Stat figure="500+" label="Artists" accent />
  <Stat figure="6" label="Cities" />
</StatRow>
```

Figure is the graffiti tag face (`--gp-font-graffiti`); label is Space Mono uppercase. Hairline divides the cells — never box them. On the live site these are the `18 / 500+ / 6 / 2008` numbers.
