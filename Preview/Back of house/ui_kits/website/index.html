The original

Two decades in, and still perfecting it. It's our most authentic experience yet — and we're still pushing.

Graffiti+ started in Vancouver in 2008 — not as a product, but as a question: what happens when you put real spray-can tools in people's hands, in public, at scale?

Since then we've run 500+ activations on six continents for brands including Nike, Chanel, Hennessy, Porsche, Google, and IBM. The technology has evolved. The premise hasn't.

How it works
