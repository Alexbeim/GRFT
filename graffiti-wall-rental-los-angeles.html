FAQ

LA questions.

Do you have a local crew in Los Angeles?

Yes. Local LA graffiti writers work with us year-round — they staff the wall, guide guests, and bring the city's own style to every activation. No fly-in crew reading from a manual.

Where in LA do you set up?

Anywhere the event is. We've run walls at Corey Helford Gallery in DTLA, Anime Expo at the Convention Center, brand spaces across the city, and trade show booths at OFC. Galleries, studio lots, premieres, rooftops — if there's power and floor space, we're in.

Does it work outdoors in full sun?

Yes. The LED configuration is built for daylight — modular panels that stay fully visible in direct California sun. LCD and projection setups work best indoors or after dark.

How far in advance should we book?

4–6 weeks ahead is comfortable for LA events. Our gear and crew are frequently in the city, so shorter timelines are often workable — ask.
