FAQ

Asia questions.

Which countries in Asia do you cover?

We've delivered activations in Japan (Tokyo), South Korea (Seoul), Singapore, Hong Kong, Macau and Indonesia (Jakarta) — and we ship the platform anywhere in the region. If your event is in Asia, we can be there.

Do you bring local graffiti artists?

Wherever possible, yes. Part of what makes the experience real is a local writer facilitating it — from Tokyo to Macau, we work with artists from the local scene rather than flying in a fixed crew.

Can you handle logistics and customs into the region?

Yes. We've shipped and run the full rig across Asia for festivals, brand launches, conventions and permanent LED installations. Send us your dates and venue and we'll plan the logistics with you.

What kinds of events is it right for in Asia?

Brand launches, product activations, street-art and music festivals, conventions, mall and casino landmark installations, and cultural events. From a 98″ LCD in a hotel lobby to a landmark LED facade, we scale to the space.
